Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer same-day delivery?

add

Yes, we offer same-day delivery across London for orders placed before 1:00 PM GMT. Our dedicated couriers ensure your botanical arrangements arrive in pristine condition.

Can I customize my bouquet?

add

Absolutely. Our florists specialize in bespoke arrangements. You can specify preferred flowers, colors, and styles through our contact form or by visiting our studio in person.

How do I care for my flowers?

add

Every Kngu arrangement comes with a detailed care guide. Generally, we recommend trimming stems at an angle, using fresh room-temperature water, and keeping them away from direct sunlight.
